I recently cracked (A) Three Friends from Codeforces Round 605 (Div. 3). This problem is a brilliant example of how a simple geometric observation can completely replace brute-force simulation. Here is a quick breakdown of the optimal approach! 💡

🛠️ The Approach
The problem asks us to minimize the total pairwise distance between three friends who can each move up to one step left, right, or stay still. Instead of brute-forcing all 27 possible combinations of their moves, we can reduce the logic to a single mathematical formula:
Fast I/O: sys.stdin.read().split() handles the input stream instantly, avoiding the overhead of repetitive I/O calls for the $q$ test cases.
The Geometric Insight: If you place any three points on a 1D line (let's sort them as $x \le y \le z$), the sum of their pairwise distances $(y-x) + (z-x) + (z-y)$ mathematically simplifies to exactly $2(z-x)$. This means the position of the middle friend is irrelevant to the total sum—only the distance between the outermost friends matters!
Minimizing the Gap: To make the total distance as small as possible, we simply need to move the outermost friends (the max_pos and min_pos) towards each other. Each friend can take 1 step, meaning we can reduce their total difference by exactly 2.
The Final Formula: I calculated the initial difference with diff = max_pos - min_pos. The new total distance becomes 2 * max(0, diff - 2). The max(0, ...) ensures that if the friends start at the exact same spot or only 1 step apart, the distance correctly bottoms out at 0 instead of dipping into negative numbers.

⏱️ Complexity Analysis
Time Complexity: O(1) per test case. By using pure mathematics and a couple of built-in min() and max() checks, we completely avoid looping or simulation. The overall time for $q$ queries is strictly O(Q).
Space/Memory Complexity: O(1) auxiliary space. The only memory used is for storing the output strings before printing them in a single batch.
It is always incredibly satisfying when you can bypass heavy state simulations with a clean, single-line math equation! ✅
